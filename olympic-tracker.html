Settlement summary

Per strain: sales at Nabis minus deductions and packaging costs = what goes back to Olympic.

  • Click any bubble (or strain row) to expand it and edit; the collapsed line always shows the current totals.
  • Net to Olympic per strain = gross sales − discounts − credits − Nabis fees − other − packaging labor − packaging materials.
  • When a strain has Nabis order rows, its gross / discounts / credits are computed from those rows (marked auto); Nabis fees and Other stay manual.
  • Order rows marked Nabis arrive automatically from the hourly Nabis export, matched on the batch codes in the strain row; they refresh every run and are not hand-editable.
  • Remitted = cash Nabis has actually paid out to Filifera, per order line (net of line discounts and credits), from the export's Remitted Date; pending = sold but not yet paid out. Colors: green = Olympic, amber = Filifera, blue = Nabis.
  • Budtender samples consume bulk and cost labor/materials but produce no revenue (purple Sample chip).
  • This board is shared and live: everyone with the link sees the same data (refreshes every minute); editing requires the edit key.
