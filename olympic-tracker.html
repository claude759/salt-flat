Settlement summary

Per strain: sales at Nabis minus deductions and packaging costs = what goes back to Olympic.

  • Net to Olympic per strain = gross sales − discounts − credits − Nabis fees − other − packaging labor − packaging materials.
  • When a strain has Nabis order rows, its gross / discounts / credits are computed from those rows (marked auto); Nabis fees and Other stay manual.
  • Order rows marked Nabis arrive automatically from the hourly Nabis export, matched on the batch codes listed in each strain bubble; they refresh every run and are not hand-editable.
  • Budtender samples consume bulk and cost labor/materials but produce no revenue; they are marked with a purple Sample chip.
